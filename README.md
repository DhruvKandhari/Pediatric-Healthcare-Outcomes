# 🏥 Pediatric Healthcare Outcomes – Socioeconomic Impact Study

### 👋 About This Project
This analytical study dives deep into pediatric healthcare data to uncover how socioeconomic factors—such as income, race, gender, and region—influence healthcare outcomes like neonatal deaths and substance abuse treatments.

### 🎯 What I Aimed to Achieve
- Reveal key socioeconomic disparities affecting pediatric healthcare outcomes.
- Inform impactful healthcare policies and insurance strategies.

### 🛠️ How I Did It (Skills & Tools Used)
- **Hypothesis Testing & Statistical Analysis** *(Chi-Squared, Kruskal-Wallis, Dunn’s Test)*
- **Data Processing & Analytics** *(Python, Excel, SQL)*
- **Data Visualization & Reporting** *(Tableau, Power BI, Excel)*
- **Strategic Recommendations & Insights**

### 📈 What I Found
- Significant disparities based on gender and race, with females and minority groups often facing higher treatment charges.
- Potential annual savings of approximately **$500 billion** by streamlining medical billing and administrative processes.

### 🌟 Recommendations Delivered
- Introduce targeted subsidized care programs, especially for females and minority groups.
- Advocate for stringent tobacco cessation initiatives to reduce neonatal healthcare burdens.

### 🙋‍♂️ My Role in This Project
- Performed statistical analyses and validated insights through multiple hypothesis tests.
- Created clear visualizations to communicate complex data findings effectively.
- Delivered strategic recommendations during stakeholder presentations.
